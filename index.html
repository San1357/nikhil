About

From the model notebook to the camera on the pole.

I'm a Senior Data Scientist at QuEST Global in Pune. Most of my work starts with a camera feed or a sensor signal and ends with a model running in real time on an Nvidia Jetson or Intel device.

I've built object detection, tracking and semantic segmentation systems for automotive and traffic clients, ECG arrhythmia detection for a European healthcare OEM, demand forecasting for a cafe chain, and search and topic modelling for Elsevier.

I like taking a state-of-the-art model, adapting it to a specific use case, and squeezing it until it's fast enough for the edge. I learn new tools by building with them.

ECG arrhythmia detection & severity

Classifies ECG signals as normal or arrhythmic, then scores severity by how far a signal deviates from a normal heartbeat.

  • 1D CNN with residual blocks for classification

  • Higher reconstruction deviation means higher severity
